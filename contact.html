FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment through our website by clicking the "Book Now" button, calling us directly at 9466868680, or visiting our salon in person. We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance.

What is your cancellation policy?

We understand plans change. Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment to avoid a cancellation fee. Late cancellations may incur a 50% service charge.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes! We offer gift cards in various denominations. They make perfect gifts for birthdays, anniversaries, or any special occasion. Gift cards can be purchased at the salon or through our website.

What products do you use?

We use premium organic and cruelty-free products from renowned brands. Our product line includes professional-grade hair care, skincare, and nail products that deliver exceptional results while being gentle on you and the environment.

Do you accept walk-ins?

While we recommend booking in advance to ensure availability, we do accept walk-ins based on our schedule. Please call ahead to check availability, especially during peak hours and weekends.

Is there parking available?

Yes! We have complimentary parking available for our clients in our private lot. There's also street parking available nearby. Your comfort and convenience are important to us.
